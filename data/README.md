# Data layers

- raw: licensed source files; retain originals read-only. Five category directories mirror the earlier project.
- processed: accepted local master panel and frozen prepared inputs. Files are copied, not linked to writable old paths.
- results: aggregate estimates, diagnostics and descriptive counts that can be shared.
- temp: new reconstruction and numerical checks; never replace results silently.
- manifests: size/SHA256 and availability. Manifest entries do not imply redistribution permission.

Date clocks, calibration pools and benchmarks must travel with results. O2C historical question-date outputs and C2C availability outputs must not be pooled as one specification.
